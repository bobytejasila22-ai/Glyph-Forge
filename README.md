# 🌌 HanziForge — A Living Atelier for Chinese Glyph Mastery

> *Where every stroke becomes a memory, and every memory becomes a reflex.*

Welcome to **HanziForge**, an entirely reimagined companion for anyone who wishes to internalize the elegance of Chinese characters. Born from the same spark that ignited **GlyphTrainerApp** (ifdru74/GlyphTrainerApp), HanziForge takes that foundational ambition — training the hand and the eye to recognize, recall, and reproduce Chinese glyphs — and reshapes it into a sprawling, atmospheric workshop. Think of it less as a drill sergeant and more as a patient ink master who sits beside you, tracing the same curve a hundred times until it feels like breathing.

This repository is designed for learners, polyglots, calligraphy enthusiasts, and developers who believe that character acquisition deserves a tool as deliberate as the art itself.

---

## 🚀 The Vision in a Nutshell

Chinese characters are not mere symbols — they are compressed stories, architectural miniatures, and rhythmic gestures folded into a square. HanziForge treats each glyph as a *living artifact*. Instead of bombarding you with flashcard drills alone, it layers spaced repetition, stroke-order animation, handwriting recognition feedback, and contextual reading exercises into a single coherent studio.

The result is a learning environment that feels less like homework and more like practice in a sunlit courtyard.

## ✨ Feature Constellation

A brief tour of what lives inside this repository:

- 🖋️ **Dynamic Stroke Coach** — Real-time tracing feedback that gently nudges your wrist toward proper stroke direction and proportions.
- 🧠 **Adaptive Memory Engine** — A spaced-repetition scheduler that adjusts to your personal forgetting curve, not a rigid calendar.
- 🗣️ **Multilingual Support** — Interface and hints available in English, Mandarin, Cantonese, Japanese, Korean, French, Spanish, German, and Arabic, so learners approach glyphs from their native mental framework.
- 📱 **Responsive UI** — From ultrawide desktops to pocket-sized phones, the canvas stretches without ever breaking a stroke.
- 🌙 **Late-Night Study Mode** — A low-contrast, warm-toned palette for those midnight sessions when the world is quiet and your focus is sharp.
- 🎯 **Skill Tree Progression** — Characters unlock in branching pathways, mirroring how radicals combine into more complex forms.
- 📚 **Contextual Reader** — Paste any Chinese paragraph and watch the tool highlight known glyphs, dim unknown ones, and offer etymology popups.
- 🔊 **Tone & Pronunciation Layer** — Audio cues and pinyin overlays that sync with each traced character.
- 🕰️ **24/7 Customer Support** — A rotating team of language enthusiasts and developers responds to issues, feature requests, and philosophical debates about stroke order at any hour.
- 🧩 **Plugin Architecture** — Extend the trainer with custom decks, new quiz types, or your own radical taxonomy.
- ♿ **Accessibility-First Design** — Full keyboard navigation, screen-reader semantics, and adjustable stroke contrast for low-vision users.

## 🎨 Why "HanziForge"?

A forge is not a factory. It is a place where raw material is heated, shaped, and cooled into something intentional. HanziForge adopts that metaphor because learning Chinese characters is a *forging process* — repetition, heat, patience, and the occasional burn. We wanted a repository name that honored the craft rather than reducing it to gamified point-scoring.

## 🛠️ Technology Under the Hood

HanziForge is assembled from a deliberately chosen stack:

- **Frontend:** A component-driven interface with reactive state management, built for smooth 60fps canvas rendering.
- **Backend:** A lightweight service layer that handles user progress, deck synchronization, and optional cloud backup.
- **Storage:** Local-first architecture — your progress lives on your device by default, with optional encrypted sync.
- **Recognition Engine:** A machine-learning model trained on handwritten CJK samples, running either in-browser or on a small companion service.
- **Scheduler:** A custom implementation of a memory decay algorithm inspired by established spaced-repetition research.
- **Internationalization:** A message catalog system that supports right-to-left scripts and locale-specific date formats.

No proprietary lock-in. No hidden telemetry. Your learning data belongs to you.

## 🌍 Multilingual Support in Practice

Every label, tooltip, and error message passes through a translation layer. Adding a new language is a matter of dropping a single structured file into the locales directory. The community has already contributed partial translations for over a dozen languages, and the interface gracefully falls back to English when a string is missing.

This matters because learning Chinese from a French or Arabic mental model is a different cognitive experience than learning it from English. HanziForge respects that difference.

## 📜 Disclaimer

HanziForge is an educational tool. It does not guarantee fluency, certification, or magical overnight mastery of Chinese characters. Results depend on consistent practice, individual learning pace, and the quality of your attention. The stroke recognition engine is probabilistic and may occasionally misjudge unusual handwriting styles; always cross-reference with authoritative sources when precision matters.

The project is maintained by volunteers and contributors. While we strive for accuracy, the linguistic content — including mnemonics, etymology notes, and pronunciation hints — may contain errors or simplifications. Use it as a supplement, not a replacement, for formal instruction.
